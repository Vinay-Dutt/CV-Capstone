Classical Computer Vision Module Pipeline

1. Preprocessing

Aspect-ratio preserved resizing, Grayscale mapping, Gaussian blurring, Median noise filtering, and CLAHE Adaptive Histogram Equalization.

2. Feature Extraction

Canny Edge density calculations, Contour topology mapping, GLCM Texture matrices (Contrast, Energy, Homogeneity), and Shannon Entropy metrics.

3. ICI Mathematical Model

Weighted feature fusion producing a normalized Image Complexity Index (0–100) and classification into 5 standardized levels.

4. Analytics & Reports

Dynamic Chart.js radar & bar visualizers, side-by-side comparative ranking, and automated PDF, CSV, and Excel assessment exports.
