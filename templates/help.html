Frequently Asked Questions

Classical Computer Vision algorithms (Canny, GLCM, Entropy) provide 100% deterministic, mathematically explainable metrics. Unlike deep learning black-box models, classical metrics offer full transparency into why an image receives a specific complexity score.

Supported formats include JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, and TIFF. Images up to 32MB are accepted. High-resolution images are automatically scaled to a max dimension of 1024px to maintain fast real-time processing while preserving exact aspect ratios.

Expand the Advanced Classical CV Hyperparameters accordion on the Upload page. You can adjust Gaussian Blur kernel size (3x3 to 7x7) and modify Canny Low (10-150) and High (100-255) thresholds.
